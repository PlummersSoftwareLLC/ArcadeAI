Observation and failure semantics
--------------------------------
Every role must publish OBSERVATIONS_COMPLETE, its exact role, four cases,
comparison_pass:boolean, interpretation_requires_review:boolean and
qualification_pass:false. BASE_A additionally says baseline_reference_only;
its comparison_pass:true means reference construction, not an independent test.

The worker must persist full actual native payloads, expected reference identities,
case/path/tolerance/masks/statistics and RNG evidence BEFORE assessing mismatch.
Finite, structurally valid original-tolerance mismatches continue through the
remaining cases and roles and require review; they are never silently promoted,
substituted or cured. Exact PRE RNG restoration is mandatory. POST RNG movement
or cross-role differences are retained and require review, rather than discarding
the rest of the observation. Persistent model/Adam/scaler/exposure/clocks changes,
nonfinite/structural/runtime/ownership/persistence faults remain fatal.

The parent returns OBSERVATIONS_COMPLETE only after all three complete roles and
owned cleanup; it propagates comparison/review flags and labels BASE_A reference
only. qualification_pass is ALWAYS false. Exit0 therefore means a completed
diagnostic that may contain mismatches; it never means OFF/ON parity, successful
training, source equivalence in general, or faster learning. A fatal child error
stops later roles and retains the original partial artifacts and actual exit;
it does not rerun a case or reuse any previous failed-run result.

Why no pre-load capture, and why startup8 is not steady state
------------------------------------------------------------
For the held exact-graph resume route, constructor replay.size is0 and a saved
nonempty mmap ring defers adoption to load. _exact_graph_loaded=False gates both
producer and background learner. load holds the training RLock, pauses and
invalidates prefetch before restoring model/Adam/scaler/clocks/ring/banks, then
validates exact graphs on fixed128 replay rows before declaring loaded. It
invalidates prefetch again before returning. No optimizer work on random
pre-load weights follows on this route; compile warmups do not consume ordinals.
Timeout, incompatible graph checkpoint, missing optimizer or failed graph parity
is fatal on this profile. Replay/bank load errors can still be caught and logged
while load returns True, so the separate intended-state proof above is necessary.

After successful load, the pressure rule initially permits a4096 batch when
sample/frame deltas are0. The first captured attempt can precede fleet resume.
The fixed eight are the FIRST consumed eligible train_step attempts, after
enable/replay/pressure/batch guards and BEFORE transfer. Not eight steady-state,
post-resume, successful or applied updates. Guard-rejected/invalidated leases
do not count; existing producer slots can draw more batches than get consumed.
Transfer/forward/update/readback/priority failure retains its ordinal, and actual
AMP skip is retained. Never replace a failed ordinal with a ninth attempt.

Ordinary shutdown and recovery limits
-------------------------------------
main.finally saves latest with save_replay=True BEFORE server.stop drains/stops
AsyncReplayBuffer and BEFORE agent.stop joins learner/prefetch. client_count0
can precede disconnect reset enqueue. In-flight frames, a consumer's private
batch, resets/n-step tails and bank admissions can occur after that save boundary.
The training RLock serializes the model/Adam save, but the held non-ratchet path
does not disable learning before it; further optimizer updates may occur before
agent.stop too. Thus normal persistence need not include the very last replay
tail OR every last in-memory update. Save/load bank errors can be swallowed.
An msync-disabled mmap flush is not a new power-loss durability guarantee.

This plan preserves the normal verified persisted state and insurance; it cannot
promise zero tail loss, exact model/ring/bank correspondence, actor/game/RNG
continuation or a coherent learning anchor. Existing managed-measurement acks
are evaluation-specific, not a proven ordinary shutdown barrier. Root must
accept these bounded ordinary-restart limits or stop this rollout and review a
separate drain/save design. The old coherent snapshot patch is not implicit here.

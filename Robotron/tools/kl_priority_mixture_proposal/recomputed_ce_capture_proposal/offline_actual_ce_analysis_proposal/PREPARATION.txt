Descriptions and their limits
- Actual origin boundaries define PER/interesting/recent/HOF/EPHOF; indices never
  replace origins. All duplicate row observations are retained. No persistent
  slot generation, bank CURRENT generation, conversion age or whole-tree minimum
  is fabricated. The source's tree metric identity remains explicitly unknown;
  held CE run provenance comes from the separately bound acquisition job.
- Emitted FP32 weights must equal the consumed weights and the FP32 cast of the
  captured FP64 weights. Selected-PER normalization uses the captured raw-weight
  maximum and its original1e-12 denominator floor. Recent/interesting carry1;
  bank rows carry a common same-batch mean PER weight. Float64 NumPy mean vs
  Python fsum residual is reported, not used to invent a learner tolerance.
  Original raw-weight formula residual is also descriptive metadata arithmetic.
- sum(w_i*CE_i)/4096 describes Bellman objective contribution. sum(w_i)/4096 is
  coefficient mass. (sum w)^2/sum(w^2) describes coefficient concentration; this
  is neither an independently sampled population IS ESS nor a measured gradient
  norm. Parameter Jacobians, vector directions and interactions remain unknown.
- Expert/nonexpert, actor-kind bytes0/1/2, terminal/horizon/reward-sign strata and
  the five origins are separate overlapping descriptive partitions. Origin
  contributions and coefficient masses can be compared without treating actor
  tags as an independently observed live action source (e.g. an eval label).
- The known scalar BC contribution uses the executed bc_loss and existing
  schedule/floor/expert fraction. Actual weighted loss also includes Q-policy
  and margin terms. Their combined residual includes FP32/AMP reduction effects;
  individual/per-row auxiliary contributions are NOT observed or attributed.
- The ordinary loss route returns12 diagnostic zeros. If a selected attempt is
  diagnostic_due, indices9/11 retain actual unweighted batch entropy/KL means;
  even then no per-row target, entropy, KL or ranking is observed. No executed
  per-row KL weight vector can be deduced from these batch means.
- AMP skips remain in all-eight descriptions and are labelled separately.
  applied_admission means the real call was admitted, not every parameter moved.
  Eight startup attempts are not IID, a sustained-throughput baseline, a quality
  comparison, a matched replay anchor or evidence of faster learning.
